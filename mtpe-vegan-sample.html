Step 3 · Editorial Notes

Why Each Change Was Made

A
植物性食品を食べることは、風味を諦めることを意味しません → ヴィーガン食でも、おいしさは一切妥協なし

The MT output reads like formal prose. On Instagram and TikTok, the opening line needs to hook the reader immediately. The revised version is punchy and direct. おいしさ ("deliciousness") is also more natural in Japanese SNS contexts than 風味 ("flavor"), which sounds clinical.

B
とても満足のいくもので、肉を恋しく思うことさえないでしょう → 肉がなくても大満足！

The phrase 〜もので and 〜ないでしょう are grammatically correct but register as written language — not how people talk on social media. Japanese SNS copy relies on short sentences, exclamation marks, and noun-ending structures (体言止め). 大満足！ delivers the same meaning in four characters.

C
食事の準備や平日の夜の素早い夕食に最適 → ミールプレップにも平日のさくっと夜ごはんにも◎

In the vegan and wellness community on Japanese social media, "meal prep" is widely used as a loanword (ミールプレップ). Using the established term is more natural than translating it literally. さくっと (quickly, easily) and the ◎ symbol are both common stylistic conventions in Japanese lifestyle content.

D
この投稿を保存して、今夜試してみてください！ → 保存して、今夜1品つくってみて🌱

The polite imperative 〜してください can feel stiff or demanding in casual SNS contexts. The informal 〜してみて is friendlier and more conversational. Specifying 1品 ("one dish") makes the action concrete and achievable, which strengthens the call-to-action. The leaf emoji is kept at the end to match the visual style of the target audience.
